📐 Empirical Bayes Shrinkage

T20 situations suffer from high sample noise. We pull raw per-ball net residual effects toward zero (E[μ]=0) using player exposure variance σi2 = σe2/ni, preventing small-sample outliers from skewing rankings.

🛡️ 4-Point Stability Screen

Every candidate is subjected to: (1) Cluster bootstrap CI low > 0, (2) Sensitivity grid stability (≥ 9/18 configs), (3) Top-10 in restricted elite competitions, and (4) Drop-one match resilience (≤ 15).

🎯 Batting Order Adjustment

Bowler baselines incorporate opposition batting order buckets (1–3, 4–6, 7+). This removes artificial inflation from dismissing tail-enders in dying overs and accurately credits top-order death over specialists.
